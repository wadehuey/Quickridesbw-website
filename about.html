About Quick Rides BW

A Botswana ride platform built for people.

Quick Rides BW exists to create value for drivers, passengers and local businesses. Our vision is a ride-hailing ecosystem where fair fares support sustainable driving, passengers receive a better experience, and communities benefit from local participation.

Our vision

Fair fares. Better rides. Stronger communities.

We want to build a trusted Botswana-first mobility brand that understands local operating costs, local passengers and the businesses that support drivers every day.

Our approach

Everyone should have a reason to choose Quick Rides.

  • Drivers should have sustainable earnings.
  • Passengers should receive clean, comfortable and reliable rides.
  • Local businesses should have opportunities to participate.
  • Botswana should benefit from a platform built for Botswana.
What we stand for

Local value, not just local branding.
