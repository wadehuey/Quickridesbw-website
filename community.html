Supporting local businesses

Every ride can help Botswana grow.

Quick Rides connects mobility with local economic activity — from water shops and car washes to drivers and passengers.

The local value chain

Supporting local businesses. Growing together.

The model shown on our flyer is simple and practical.

Drivers buy water locally

Quick Care Programme

Clean cars. Happy passengers. Better earnings.

We can partner with car washes and water suppliers to give drivers discounted services.

  • Discounted car washes
  • Discounted bottled water
  • Better service for passengers
  • More value for local businesses
Together, we go further

Everyone wins when the ecosystem works.

Drivers get practical support. Passengers get a better experience. Local businesses get more opportunities. Botswana gets a locally focused mobility brand.
