Services

More than a ride.

Quick Rides BW is designed to support everyday mobility and additional transport opportunities across Botswana.

🚕

Ride-hailing

Connect passengers with drivers for everyday trips using a platform focused on fair fares, clean vehicles and dependable service.

📦

Courier services

Create opportunities for drivers to support local deliveries and courier needs through the same mobility ecosystem.

🚚

Truck services

Support larger transport requirements and give suitable operators another way to access customers.

👥

Group & contract services

Transport solutions for organisations, groups and recurring journeys where a structured service is needed.

💧

Water partnerships

Connect drivers with local water shops so passengers can receive complimentary water on selected rides while shops gain business.

🚿

Quick Care Programme

Partnerships with car washes and water suppliers can provide drivers with discounted services that support cleaner cars and better passenger experiences.

Built for the ecosystem

Drivers, passengers and businesses all have a place in Quick Rides.

That is how a ride becomes more than a trip — it becomes local economic activity.
